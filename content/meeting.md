## WASI Monthly Members Meeting

Monthly members meetings are held at 7:00 PM Eastern time on the second Wednesday of the
month at the Bear Branch Nature Center, 300 John Owings Rd. Westminster, MD and
online via Zoom. There's a demo or class at 7:00 PM,
followed by the featured guest speaker presentation at 7:30 PM.

Our August 12 meeting features a talk by WASI member Alan Goldberg titled "HST Operations From an Amateur Astronomer's
POV".

Alan plans to compare how HST makes an observation to how amateurs make observations, with an emphasis on how automation was essential for HST design 48 yrs ago, and has slowly bled into the Seestar and other automated scopes.

Alan Goldberg has been been learning to be an amateur astronmers since becoming intersted interested in about about 3rd grade. He trained in astronomy, mostly at MIT. He left graduate school to work on HST at Perkin-Elmer as it began its prime contract work building the telescope. Moving to the DC area, he joined the Space Telescope Science Institute as it formed, and then worked at Goddard SFC on testing and operations planning. After a brief stint at a technology startup, he joined MITRE Corp. and worked 35 years advising the government on satellite acquisition planning and contract oversight. He resumed his amateur astronomy about 2000 and became active in NOVAC, including helping to build AHSP and serving as VP for 6 years. He fully retired last year, moved to MD, and joined WASI.
